ANIRBAN-1

IoT Mesh for Autonomous Aircraft Safety

A collaborative research and technical concept exploring advisory-only cooperative safety coordination using an IoT mesh architecture for autonomous aircraft.

Research Overview

ANIRBAN-1 proposes an advisory-only IoT mesh architecture intended to support cooperative intent sharing, fault-aware traffic coordination, and staged validation for autonomous aircraft safety.

The concept does not issue flight-control commands and does not replace existing aviation safety systems such as TCAS/ACAS.

Research Focus

IoT Mesh Architecture

Cooperative communication and real-time information exchange between participating nodes.

Fault Awareness

Consideration of message loss, stale information, network partitions, and disagreement conditions.

Cybersecurity

Consideration of message integrity, authentication, replay protection, malformed data, and compromised processes.

Staged Validation

A proposed validation pathway from software simulation through progressively higher-fidelity evaluation.

Validation Methodology

The proposed validation pathway includes model review, software simulation, network simulation, hardware-in-the-loop evaluation, human-factors assessment, aircraft feasibility studies, and only later stages subject to appropriate approvals.

Research Concept

The project identifies research questions around prediction lead time, message-loss conditions, uncertainty-aware sensing, operator differentiation of safe and unsafe proposals, and measurable benefit while considering false alarms and legacy aircraft.

Important Disclaimer

ANIRBAN-1 is a research concept and proposed validation framework. It is not presented as a demonstrated safety improvement, certified aviation system, or replacement for existing operational safety systems.

The proposed contributions and technical claims require experimental evidence and staged validation.
